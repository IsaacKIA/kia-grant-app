# KIA-START UP CONSULT — Client Grant Management & Readiness Suite

A dedicated, full-featured web application designed for **Kia-Start Up Consult** (Accra, Ghana) to assess, diagnose, match, and manage startups through the entire 7-stage grant acquisition pipeline.

---

## 🚀 Key Modules Built In

### 1. 7-Stage Grant Pipeline CRM
* **Kanban & Table Views**: Track startups across all 7 stages:
  1. *Onboarding & GHS 200 Commitment Fee*
  2. *Documentation & Preparation*
  3. *Grant Search & Matching*
  4. *Application Development*
  5. *Submission & Tracking*
  6. *Grant Outcome & Success Fee %* (features award celebration animations)
  7. *Post-Award Support*
* **Detailed Checklist & Red Flags**: Interactive checklist modeled on the Notion dashboard with risk alerts for communication delays, compliance issues, and document gaps.

### 2. Automated Grant Readiness Calculator (0–100 Scale)
* **10 Pillars & 20 Concrete Sub-Criteria**: Reconciles the 0–5 scoring guide with the 100-point total.
* **Instant Dynamic Scoring**: Color-coded score dial (🟢 Highly Ready, 🟡 Moderately Ready, 🟠 Low Readiness, 🔴 Not Ready).
* **Automated Gap Analysis & Action Roadmap**: Automatically highlights every weak metric (<4/5) with specific remediation steps.

### 3. Intelligent Grant Matcher
* Catalog of top funding opportunities across Ghana and West Africa:
  * *GEA / World Bank YouStart*
  * *Mastercard Foundation / Agri-Impact (HAPPY Project)*
  * *NEIP Innovation Challenge Fund*
  * *Tony Elumelu Foundation (TEF)*
  * *USAID Feed the Future Ghana Activity*
  * *GIZ / develoPPP Ventures*
  * *Kosmos Innovation Center (KIC)*
* Cross-references startup sector, target grant size, and minimum readiness score.

### 4. Official Printable Diagnostic Report (PDF)
* Generates an executive, branded **Kia-Start Up Consult Grant Readiness Audit** formatted for A4 printing and direct sharing with founders.
